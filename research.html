DRONES · A FLIGHT HISTORY

From WWI target aircraft to survey-grade mapping UAVs. A short history of unmanned flight, a gallery of the airframes that shaped it, and a close look at two working Indian platforms this project borrows its airframe designs from: the ideaForge Q6 and the Asteria Aerospace surveillance line.

Timeline

How unmanned flight got here

A real chronological sequence — each stage builds capability the next one depends on.

01 · 1917–1918

First powered UAVs

Experimental unmanned aircraft like the Kettering Bug were built as unpiloted "aerial torpedoes" — crude by later standards, but the first proof that an aircraft could fly a preset course with no one aboard.

02 · 1930s–1950s

Radio-controlled targets

Militaries built radio-controlled aircraft such as the de Havilland Queen Bee to train anti-aircraft gunners, coining the term "drone" for a remotely flown target.

03 · 1960s–1990s

Reconnaissance era

Jet-powered reconnaissance drones like the Ryan Firebee family flew surveillance missions where a piloted aircraft was too risky to send.

04 · 1995

The armed UAV arrives

The General Atomics Predator entered service as a surveillance platform and was later adapted to carry weapons, changing how militaries thought about unmanned aircraft entirely.

05 · 2010

Consumer drones begin

The Parrot AR.Drone let anyone fly a quadcopter from a smartphone over Wi-Fi, moving UAV technology out of military and industrial hands for the first time.

06 · 2013–present

Mainstream aerial imaging

DJI's Phantom and later Mavic lines put stabilized 4K cameras on folding airframes, and aerial photography became a mainstream hobby and profession.

07 · 2018–present

Enterprise & survey drones

Purpose-built mapping and surveillance UAVs — including the Indian-built ideaForge Q6 and Asteria Aerospace fleet — moved drones into agriculture, construction, disaster response and border security.

08 · Ongoing

Autonomy & swarms

Current development is pushing toward autonomous mission planning, obstacle avoidance and coordinated multi-drone flight — the direction this simulator's level design is modeled after.

Gallery

Famous drones worth knowing

A working pilot's shortlist — spanning military reconnaissance, consumer photography and enterprise survey work.

Military · 1917

Kettering Bug

An early American unmanned "aerial torpedo," steered by a preset number of engine revolutions rather than any onboard guidance.

Reconnaissance · 1951

Ryan Firebee

A jet-powered target and reconnaissance drone whose airframe was adapted across decades of Cold War surveillance missions.

Military · 1995

MQ-1 Predator

A General Atomics UAV that began as a surveillance aircraft and became the first drone widely adapted to carry precision weapons.

Military · 1998

RQ-4 Global Hawk

A high-altitude, long-endurance surveillance drone able to stay aloft for over a day, covering vast areas without landing.

Consumer · 2010

Parrot AR.Drone

The first quadcopter widely flown by ordinary people, piloted entirely from a smartphone screen over Wi-Fi.

Consumer · 2013

DJI Phantom

A ready-to-fly quadcopter line that put a stabilized camera in the hands of hobbyists and mainstreamed aerial photography.

Enterprise · 2020

ideaForge Switch

A folding VTOL surveillance UAV built for over two hours of endurance, used in inspection and rescue operations across India.

ideaForge Q6 & Asteria

The two platforms this project's "Falcon Q6" and "Asteria Recon" airframes are styled after. Full specs in the deep-dive sections below.

Deep Dive · Survey Drone

ideaForge Q6

A small-category quadcopter built by the Indian manufacturer ideaForge for large-area mapping and survey work. It launches and lands itself vertically, so it needs no runway, and it is light enough to be classified as a small-category drone under Indian drone rules while still covering large survey areas per flight.

Weight class

Under 6 kg

Loaded weight including battery and payload stays under 6 kg, keeping it in the small-drone category.

Endurance

Up to ~50–60 minutes

Long enough per flight to cover several thousand square kilometres of survey ground over a full mission.

Launch & recovery

Autonomous VTOL

Takes off and lands itself vertically — no catapult, runway or net needed in the field.

Mapping accuracy

~5 cm ground sampling distance

Its photogrammetry payload, paired with a PPK positioning module, resolves ground detail to roughly 5 cm per pixel.

Payload options

HD, thermal & EO/IR

Swappable daylight, thermal and dual EO/IR payloads adapt it for mapping, night surveillance or public-safety response.

Operating envelope

-20°C to +50°C, 10 m/s wind

Rated for harsh field conditions, from cold mountain surveys to hot, dusty farmland, with meaningful wind tolerance.

Used for large-scale land mapping, glacial and avalanche-risk surveys in the Himalayas, and — in a later "V3" variant — public-safety missions such as border monitoring, firefighting support and disaster response.

Deep Dive · Surveillance Fleet

Asteria Aerospace

An Indian drone manufacturer building rugged, camera-equipped UAVs for defence, homeland security, agriculture and infrastructure work. Its aircraft favour durability and long surveillance endurance over speed, and its software platform turns raw flights into actionable aerial intelligence rather than just video.

A200-XT

Mini survey drone

A compact, DGCA-certified airframe aimed at crop and farm-level field analysis.

A400

Surveillance quad

Carries dual optical and infrared payloads for day/night patrol, reconnaissance and forest monitoring.

A410-XT

Extended-role variant

An extended version of the A-series built for longer or more demanding surveillance sorties.

AT-15

Tactical airframe

A DGCA type-certified platform positioned for tactical and security-focused deployments.

Roughly ten years in operation with several hundred aircraft fielded, Asteria's drones support 360° aerial reconnaissance for border security and asset protection, while its SkyDeck platform and Drone-as-a-Service offering manage flight data for agriculture, energy, mining and construction clients.
