Mission Setup

SELECT LEVEL / AIRFRAME / ZONE

Every zone is its own live environment page. Your level sets the altitude band and obstacle density, your airframe sets top speed and handling — carry both into whichever zone you launch.

Step 1

Difficulty level

Levels are a true sequence — each one raises the operating altitude band and thins out margin for error.

Step 2

Choose your airframe

Each airframe changes the simulator's speed and altitude ceiling read-outs in every zone.

Step 3

Pick a zone & launch

Each card opens a fully separate live environment page carrying your level and airframe with it.
