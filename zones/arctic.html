Zone Briefing

Arctic Ridge

Glacier peaks under an aurora sky. Gates sit at staggered heights along the ridge line, so this zone is really a test of altitude discipline — the windchill knocks the airframe off its target band constantly, and the ceiling stat becomes the number to watch above all others.

Terrain

Glacier ridge line

Staggered peak heights force constant altitude correction.

Hazard

Windchill drift

Cold gusts push altitude off-band — the longest run of the four zones.

Objective

3 gates, 3.1 km

The endurance test of the set — hold your band the whole way.
