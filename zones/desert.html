Zone Briefing

Desert Basin

Wide, obstacle-free dune fields under a heat-shimmer haze. Thermals push the airframe off its line, so the drill here is holding a speed line through three gates rather than dodging clutter. Best flown low on Level 1 for wide, sweeping gate spacing, or Level 3 for a tight high-altitude crossing above the ridge.

Terrain

Open dune basin

Minimal obstacles, long sightlines, ideal for top-speed runs.

Hazard

Thermal haze drift

Rising heat currents nudge altitude — watch the ceiling stat, not just the stick.

Objective

3 gates, 2.6 km

Clear all three rings before distance-remaining hits zero.
