Zone Briefing

Metro Skyline

A night run between office towers. Gates sit between buildings instead of in open air, so the gate count climbs to four and the margin for a wide turn disappears fast — this is the zone built to reward the precision-focused airframes over the speed-focused ones.

Terrain

Tower canyon

Gates threaded between rooftops and street canyons after dark.

Hazard

Corridor pinch

Less lateral room than any other zone — overshoot and you clip a tower face.

Objective

4 gates, 1.9 km

Shorter run, tighter tolerances — precision over raw speed.
